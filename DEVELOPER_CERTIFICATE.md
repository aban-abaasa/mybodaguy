### Platform Overview: Bodagoera

**Bodagoera** is an innovative transportation platform that connects riders with motorcycle (boda boda) drivers, transforming urban mobility and logistics through technology:

- **Ride Booking System** - Real-time motorcycle ride requests
- **Driver Management** - Driver profiles and fleet management
- **Route Optimization** - Location-based matching and navigation
- **Payment Integration** - Multiple payment options including ICAN wallet
- **Journey Booking** - Advanced trip planning and scheduling
- **Company Transport** - Corporate worker transport wallets
- **SupermarketEra Integration** - Delivery services for retail
- **PWA Support** - Installable mobile application
