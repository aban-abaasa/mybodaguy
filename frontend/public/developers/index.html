For businesses

    
Your own data, behind a key you control.

    
A business owner mints a key inside ICAN, for one business, with only the powers it needs, and revokes it in a tap. There is no review queue and no one else can see or widen it.

    

      
1 · OWNERMint a key in ICANOpen your business, then Developer API. Pick the scopes and an expiry (up to a year).

      
2 · TESTRehearse safelyMake a test key: it answers with fixtures and creates nothing real.

      
3 · BUILDCall from your serverSend the key as Bearer era_biz_…. Writes carry an Idempotency-Key.

      
4 · CONTROLSee and revokeEvery call is listed for you. Revoke in one tap; it dies at once.

Requests, not transfers

        
Two parts of the platform touch real money and real people, so the API only asks:

        
Payment requests. You create one with an amount and a reference. The payer sees it in their ICAN app and approves with their own PIN. The API cannot pay, send or withdraw, and there is no endpoint that does.

        
Rides and deliveries. You get a firm quote from the same maths the app uses, then create a booking request with a confirm link. The customer opens it, signs in to BodaGoEra and books it themselves. Nothing is dispatched until they do.

      

    

    

      
Guard rails that are always on

      
    
        
  • A key belongs to exactly one business. It can never read another.
    • 
        
  • Keys are stored only as hashes, shown once, expire, and revoke instantly.
    • 
        
  • Prices come from us. Any fare you send in a booking is ignored.
    • 
        
  • Per-key caps on amount and on open requests; 5 new keys an hour, 10 active per business.
    • 
        
  • Retries are safe: the same Idempotency-Key returns the first answer.
    • 
        
  • Customer and rider identities are never returned to a business.
    • 
        
  • An admin kill switch for the whole business API, one endpoint, or payments only.
    • 
        
  • Optionally, payment keys need a verified business (set by the platform team).
    •

Reference

    
Everything, generated from the live registry.

    
This list is read from the API itself, so it can never drift from what is actually deployed.


    

      
Authenticate
Send your key in a header. Never in the URL — URLs end up in logs.
Authorization: Bearer era_test_…
# or
X-API-Key: era_test_…

      
Read the answer
Success wraps the payload; errors always have a stable code.
{ "data": …,
  "meta": { "mode": "sandbox", "ms": 3 } }
{ "error": { "code": "rate_limited",
  "message": "…", "status": 429 } }

      
Stay inside limits
Sandbox: 30 requests a minute. Live: 60 a minute by default, raised on request. Watch X-RateLimit-Remaining and honour Retry-After.
X-RateLimit-Limit: 60
X-RateLimit-Remaining: 58
X-Quota-Remaining: 4990
Retry-After: 12   (on 429)

    


    


    
Errors

    

      
HTTPcodeWhat happened

      
        
400bad_requestA parameter is missing or invalid. The message says which.

        
401missing_key invalid_key key_revokedNo key, a malformed or unknown key, or one that was rotated or revoked.

        
403scope_missing live_not_approved app_suspended business_suspendedYour key is not approved for that app, lacks the scope an endpoint needs, or the app was paused.

        
404unknown_endpoint not_foundNo such path, or nothing matched (an unknown barcode, country…).

        
405method_not_allowedUse GET for data. Only the few request-creating endpoints take POST.

        
409idempotency_conflictYou reused an Idempotency-Key with a different request. Use a fresh key.

        
413 / 415payload_too_large unsupported_media_typeBodies are JSON and up to 16 KB.

        
422unprocessableWell formed but not allowed: above your key's cap, business not verified, too many open requests.

        
429rate_limited quota_exceeded too_many_bad_keysSlow down. Retry-After tells you how long.

        
5xxhandler_error endpoint_disabled api_disabledOur side, or switched off for now. The details are logged for the team, not shown to you.

What this API will never do

      
    
        
  • Move money. A payment request only asks; the payer approves in their app with their own PIN. There is no endpoint that pays, sends or withdraws.
    • 
        
  • Book a ride on someone's behalf. A booking request is a link the customer opens and confirms themselves.
    • 
        
  • Let one business see another. A business key is bound to one business, forever.
    • 
        
  • Show anyone's wallet, balance, transactions or messages. Not even the business's own.
    • 
        
  • Hand out contact details. Listings and stores show no phone numbers or emails. People connect inside the apps.
    • 
        
  • Reveal cost prices, stock levels or suppliers to the public. Those are visible only to the business that owns them, through its own key.
    • 
        
  • Keep your key. Keys are stored only as hashes and shown once. Lose one? Rotate it.
    • 
      

      
Every call is rate limited, abusive callers are throttled, and the platform team can switch any single endpoint — or the whole API — off instantly.
